How to search your old ChatGPT conversations

You know you solved this exact problem in a chat a few weeks ago. Here is how to get back to it.

1. Use the built-in search first

ChatGPT has a search box in the sidebar that looks through your chat titles and, in many cases, the text inside them. Try two or three distinct words you remember from the conversation, not a full sentence. It is quick, and it works well when the chat is recent and titled sensibly.

2. Request your data export

In settings, under data controls, you can export your account data. It arrives as a file by email, usually containing every conversation. It is thorough, but it is a big file that is awkward to search by meaning, and it is a snapshot. The next chat you have is not in it.

3. Keep your own searchable archive

The problem with the first two is that the chats stay inside one product and you only find them by remembering the right word. A saved archive on your own device fixes both. Every time you finish a conversation worth keeping, you save it once. Later you search across all of them in one place, including chats from other assistants and the web pages you read around the same time.

Stash does this with one click from a small pill on the chat. Search runs on your device, so nothing you saved is uploaded anywhere to be indexed.

Habits that make old chats easier to find

  • Rename a chat the moment it becomes useful. A title like "pricing page copy v3" beats "New chat".
  • Save the good ones when they finish, not later. Later is when you forget.
  • Put the decision at the end of the chat: ask for a two line summary, and search for that.
